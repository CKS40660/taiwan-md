# Chen Yu-hsun: The Warm Magician of Taiwan Comedy Film

From 1995's _Tropical Fish_ to 2020's _My Missing Valentine_, Chen Yu-hsun used five feature films to establish a creative grammar for Taiwan local comedy cinema. His films are filled with Taiwanese-style humor and humanistic warmth, adept at exploring profound life issues in absurd situations. _My Missing Valentine_ won Best Narrative Feature at the 57th Golden Horse Awards, the most powerful external validation of this grammar.

## A Military Dependents' Village Boy's Film Dream

Chen Yu-hsun (born 28 May 1960) was born in Taipei; his father was a serviceman, and he grew up in a military dependents' village environment. The mixed cultural backgrounds of immigrants from various provinces and the rich human warmth of the military dependents' village provided concrete material for his later creations.

He began engaging with film during middle school, deeply influenced by Hollywood comedy masters such as Billy Wilder and Frank Capra. He discovered that film could convey deep emotions through laughter, and decided to make this his life's vocation.

He graduated from the National Taiwan University of Arts (formerly the National Taiwan Academy of Arts)[^1], receiving a complete film education. During university he shot multiple short films, displaying his talent for and love of the comedy genre.

## Creative Training in the Advertising World

After graduation Chen Yu-hsun entered the advertising industry. The fast pace and creative demands of advertising concretely trained his ability to precisely control rhythm and visual effects, leaving clear traces in his later film creations.

During his advertising years, Chen Yu-hsun directed countless creative commercials, winning numerous advertising awards. He learned to seize audience attention in the shortest time, a skill extremely practical in film creation.

In the early 1990s Taiwan's advertising industry was in an expansion phase. Chen Yu-hsun and many contemporary creators such as Tsai Ming-liang and Yi Chih-yen all moved from advertising into film, forming a new generation of Taiwan cinema.

## Film Debut: _Tropical Fish_

In 1995 Chen Yu-hsun released his film debut _Tropical Fish_. This low-budget production created a miracle in Taiwan film history. The film uses absurd humor to depict a down-on-his-luck man who becomes unexpectedly entangled in a kidnapping case.

_Tropical Fish_'s success lies in its unique Taiwanese-style humor. Chen Yu-hsun cleverly employs Taiwan social cultural elements, creating a comedy genre with local characteristics. Although the characters face predicaments, they maintain an optimistic attitude throughout.

The film won Best Original Screenplay at the 32nd Golden Horse Awards and achieved box-office success[^4]. _Tropical Fish_ proved the market potential of Taiwan local comedy film, opening a path for subsequent creators.

## Exploration of Romantic Comedy

After _Tropical Fish_, Chen Yu-hsun continued exploring different types of comedy film. 1997's _Love Comes_ attempted the romantic comedy form; although box-office performance was mediocre, it displayed his profound understanding of female characters.

2000's _Love You, Love Me_ adopted more mature narrative techniques, exploring modern urbanites' romantic dilemmas. Chen Yu-hsun began fusing more life reflections into comedy, showing the director's growing mastery.

Although works from this period lacked _Tropical Fish_'s sensational impact, they accumulated sufficient creative experience for Chen Yu-hsun and gave him a deeper understanding of comedy film's essence.

## Return to Glory: _The Chef_

After years of quiet cultivation, Chen Yu-hsun released _The Chef_ in 2013, again proving his creative strength. Set against the backdrop of Tainan's banquet catering culture, the film depicts three chefs competing for the status of "head chef."

_The Chef_'s success lies in its deep excavation of Taiwan traditional culture. Chen Yu-hsun presents the essence of banquet culture through comedy, letting young audiences recognize the value of traditional craft.

The film assembled a star cast including Joseph Chang and [Brigitte Lin](/en/people/brigitte-lin-legendary-actress/), achieving a good balance between commercial appeal and artistry. _The Chef_ was not only a box-office success but also won critical acclaim.

## Magical Realism: _My Missing Valentine_

2020's _My Missing Valentine_ is Chen Yu-hsun's most ambitious work, blending romance, comedy, magical realism, and other elements. The film depicts a fantastical story of the male protagonist searching for true love amid spacetime chaos.

The film was released during the COVID-19 pandemic; its spacetime-interweaving concept instead generated special contemporary significance. Chen Yu-hsun deftly combines sci-fi elements with Taiwanese-style comedy, creating a unique viewing experience.

_My Missing Valentine_ won five major awards including Best Narrative Feature at the 57th Golden Horse Awards, the peak of Chen Yu-hsun's career[^3]. This work proved the international competitiveness of Taiwan local film.

## Construction of Taiwanese-Style Humor

Chen Yu-hsun's most identifiable contribution is constructing a "Taiwanese-style humor" film language. His comedy takes human warmth as its texture; absurd situations are merely the shell, while the core is meticulous observation of Taiwan society and culture.

He excels at discovering absurd elements in daily life, presenting social phenomena through exaggeration. This humor style both entertains audiences and provokes reflection on reality.

Chen Yu-hsun's character portrayal pays special attention to figures' innate kindness. Even antagonists reveal the complexity of human nature, avoiding stereotyped treatment.

## Attention to Taiwan Culture

Chen Yu-hsun's films consistently attend to the preservation and transmission of Taiwan local culture. From _Tropical Fish_'s urban marginal figures to _The Chef_'s traditional crafts, he uses film to record the diverse faces of Taiwan society.

He particularly values the use of dialects, regarding language as an important cultural vehicle. In his films, Taiwanese Hokkien, Hakka, and other dialects all receive due respect and representation.

Chen Yu-hsun also attends to socially disadvantaged groups; his films often center on little people, displaying their resilience and kindness. This humanistic concern is a key characteristic of his works.

## Collaboration with Actors

Chen Yu-hsun excels at discovering and nurturing actors; many non-professional actors have delivered outstanding performances under his direction. He believes genuine emotion matters more than polished technique.

He has established long-term collaborative relationships with comedy actors such as Kuo Tzu-chien and Peng Chia-chia, forming a unique performance style. These actors all shine in his films.

Chen Yu-hsun also values cultivating newcomers, giving young actors opportunities to perform. Quite a few actors first showed promise in his works, leading to subsequent development opportunities.

## Production Philosophy and Industry Reflection

As a director, Chen Yu-hsun also cares about Taiwan film industry development. He insists on a localization path, believing Taiwan film should leverage its own cultural advantages.

He advocates refinement in low-budget production, proving that good creativity and execution matter more than massive investment. This philosophy provides a viable development direction for Taiwan independent film.

Chen Yu-hsun also actively participates in film industry discussions, offering professional opinions for Taiwan film policy formulation. He hopes to create a better environment for young creators to display their talents.

## International Vision and Local Roots

Although deeply engaged in local subjects, Chen Yu-hsun has always maintained an international vision. His works have attracted attention at overseas film festivals, proving the international appeal of Taiwan culture.

He believes the more local, the more international; Taiwan film need not deliberately cater to the international market but should display its own unique cultural characteristics.

_My Missing Valentine_'s success in the international market verified his creative philosophy. Taiwan film can gain international recognition while maintaining local characteristics.

## Influence on Young Directors

Chen Yu-hsun's success has encouraged many young directors to engage in comedy film creation. He proved Taiwan audiences' demand for and support of quality local comedy.

He also enjoys sharing creative experience, frequently participating in film education activities. Many young directors have drawn inspiration from his works and philosophy.

The Taiwan-style comedy film tradition Chen Yu-hsun established provides Taiwan cinema with a verifiable development path — not relying on big budgets, but winning through cultural rootedness and emotional authenticity.

## Current Creative Status

In recent years Chen Yu-hsun continues focusing on the comedy line, adhering to local subjects. He believes the more concrete the Taiwan details, the more likely they are to resonate with international audiences — a proposition already verified by _My Missing Valentine_'s overseas release reactions.

He simultaneously attends to talent cultivation in the Taiwan film industry, believing that giving young directors opportunities to practice is more direct than any policy subsidy. His film language excels in concreteness, letting audiences feel Taiwan for themselves in a banquet table spread or a back alley, not through slogans.

Thirty years of creation later, Chen Yu-hsun's films remain identifiable because he has always placed Taiwan's concrete life at the story's core.
