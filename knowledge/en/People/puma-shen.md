description: 'In 2021, Shen Po-yang co-founded Doublethink Lab with strategic scholar Ho Cheng-hui, supported by a NT$600 million donation from Tung Chine-cheng, aiming to train three million civil defense volunteers within three years. In February 2024, he entered the legislature as a Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) at-large legislator. On October 28, 2025, he became the first elected Taiwanese political figure to be investigated by China under the charge of "subverting state power," with CCTV running a "deep dive" special warning that "you are next." On New Year''s Day 2026, a Chinese Weibo account used commercial satellite imagery to publish the coordinates of his residence and workplace in Taipei; he spent Valentine''s Day on a flight to France. On May 13, the DPP officially mobilized him to run for Mayor of Taipei—the very city marked by those satellite coordinates.'

# Shen Po-yang: He Studied China's Cognitive Warfare, Then China Mapped Him on Satellite Imagery

One afternoon in the 2010s, a law PhD student in his early thirties followed a warden into the Taipei Juvenile Reformatory. The floor had just been mopped, and the water streaks had not yet dried. The warden stepped across; the visiting group followed. He stood still and looked around. Two teenagers who were mopping the floor looked up, bowed to him, and said softly, "Thank you." [^1]

Returning home that day, he wrote a question in his notebook: "Why is there such a huge difference in how we treat people in different places?" [^1] This question eventually led him from criminal law to criminology, from academia to civil defense, and from researching China's cognitive warfare to being investigated by Chinese public security, being warned by CCTV that "you are next," and having his home coordinates in Taipei published by a Chinese Weibo account using commercial satellites.

His name is Shen Po-yang. His stage name is Puma Shen. Born in Taipei in 1982, he is an Associate Professor and Director of the Institute of Criminology at National Taipei University. [^2] He studies the CCP's information warfare, cognitive warfare, and transborder repression against Taiwan. He studied these things for seven years; starting in the eighth year, these things began to find him instead.

> **30-Second Overview:** Shen Po-yang (born 1982) is an Associate Professor and Director of the Institute of Criminology at National Taipei University. In 2021, he co-founded [Black Bear Academy](https://kuma-academy.org/) with strategic scholar Ho Cheng-hui; Tsao Ching-cheng donated NT$600 million to support it, aiming to train three million "Black Bear Warriors" within three years. [^3] In February 2024, he was elected as a Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) at-large legislator. [^4] Starting from October 2024, he was sanctioned by China six times within one year. On October 28, 2025, he became the first elected Taiwanese politician to be investigated by China for "crimes of splitting the state." [^5] On November 9, CCTV aired a seven-and-a-half-minute exposé warning, "you are next." [^6] On New Year's Day 2026, a Chinese Weibo account used commercial satellites to publish the coordinates of his home and workplace in Taipei. [^7] He continues to fly to The Hague, Germany, and France; he spent Valentine's Day 2026 on a plane to France. [^8] In May, the DPP officially drafted him to run for Mayor of Taipei, facing Chiang Wan-an, who is seeking re-election. [^66]

## "Do Not Interrupt or Refute the Teacher While They Are Speaking"

In Shen Po-yang's elementary school communication book, a teacher once wrote: "**Do not interrupt or refute the teacher while they are speaking. Bo-yang is not bad by nature, but occasionally loses control and acts as if no one else matters.**" His mother replied below: "**His attitude is indeed poor; he acts as if no one else matters.**" In junior high school, he once flipped his teachers off. [^1] The year he was interviewed by _Mirror Media_ at age thirty-seven, he still sported an afro, a T-shirt, and shorts. His favorite character is Yang Guo—"**Total rebellion; I like this version of myself best.**" [^1]

He attended Fuxing Kindergarten, Fuxing Elementary School, and Fuxing Junior High School, before graduating from Chien Kuo High School and passing the entrance exam for the Department of Law at National Taiwan University. [^9] He noticed the existence of social class as early as junior high: "Students from Fuxing would discuss newly opened Japanese restaurants; students from Chien Kuo would eat pork chop bento boxes, while they would eat brown sugar ice—ordering plain shaved ice with just sugar water added." [^1] During his university years, he began his career in the cram school industry, using the stage name "Puma" because it was easier to remember. [^10] This stage name has followed him for twenty years—from the cram school industry and academia to think tanks and the Legislative Yuan. All of his current official social media accounts use [@pumashen](https://www.threads.com/@pumashen). [^11]

Two specific scenes caused him to shift from a "legal professional specializing in criminal law" toward criminology.

The first scene was a case involving a senior's warehouse club client. The senior's client was a large-scale retailer suing a fruit farmer. "The farmer was terrified and agreed to a settlement, but the amount was so high that it essentially destroyed an entire family." The senior said to Shen Po-yang: "That is just how the law is written; who told him to break it?" [^1] Shen Po-yang wrote: "**If you study law for too long, you really might become the very person you never intended to be.**" [^1]

The second scene was mopping the floor at a juvenile detention center. When a youth bowed to him, he couldn't help but think: the same teenager is someone being pursued on the outside, but here, they are someone to be pitied; the scale we use to view a person determines what we do to them.

This sense of proportion likely began on that day at the juvenile detention center.

## Puma and the Choice to Leave the United States Twice

Shen Po-yang's academic timeline is as simple as a flight stub. LL.B. in Law from National Taiwan University (2004), LL.M. in Law from the University of Pennsylvania (2007), LL.M. in Criminal Law from National Taiwan University (2008), and a PhD in Criminology, Law, and Society from the University of California, Irvine (UC Irvine) in 2017. His doctoral dissertation advisors were two heavyweight scholars in white-collar crime research, Henry Pontell and Elliott Currie. [^12]

During his five years in Irvine, he lived in a city ranked "number one for public safety in the U.S."—"The streets all look the same, which confuses thieves, so you don't have to lock your doors." [^1] Upon graduating in 2017, he received offers for tenure-track positions from American universities, including North Carolina State University. [^13] His five oral examination professors advised him to return to Taiwan: "You care so much about Taiwan, why not go back?" [^1]

His own explanation for choosing to return to Taiwan was brief: "**The air isn't great anyway; I've been exposed to pollution since I was a kid, and I've eaten my share of phthalates. It is my own home, after all.**" [^1]

He returned to Taipei in 2017 and joined the Institute of Criminology at National Taipei University as an assistant professor. In August 2023, he assumed the role of director. [^2] The university's official page lists his research areas as criminal law, sociology of law, criminal policy, and white-collar crime. [^2] His doctoral training was not strictly within a law school—UC Irvine trained him within the School of Social Ecology, which spans criminology, urban planning, public policy, and environmental psychology. [^14] This training later determined his perspective on researching the CCP's cognitive warfare—rather than approaching it through legal statutes, he approaches it through "how people are influenced by their environment."

Two years later, he began to turn this perspective toward another direction.

In 2018, Puma Shen began systematically collecting evidence regarding the CCP's information warfare against Taiwan. He deconstructed the CCP's United Front tactics against Taiwan into five lines: the Ministry of State Security, the United Front Work Department, the Taiwan Affairs Office, the People's Liberation Army, and the Communist Youth League. [^15] Each line has its own budget, tools, and rhythm. This analytical framework later became the standard language for civil society in Taiwan to understand cognitive warfare.

In 2019, he co-founded [Doublethink Lab](https://doublethinklab.org/) alongside civil researchers and served as its chairperson. [^16] On April 25, 2022, they released the [China Index](https://china-index.io/)—a "China influence index" covering over 80 countries across nine dimensions (media, arts, economy, society and culture, military, law enforcement, technology, politics, and diplomacy) with a total of 99 indicators. [^17] To this day, this index remains one of the most frequently cited databases regarding the CCP's external influence in international political and media circles. Using this data as a foundation, Puma Shen later testified before the [United States-China Economic and Security Review Commission](https://www.uscc.gov/sites/default/files/2023-03/Puma_Shen_Testimony.pdf) (USCC) in the United States in 2023. [^18]

In July 2024, Shen Po-yang and Doublethink Lab researcher Wu Ming-hsuan co-published [《打台灣不如騙台灣：中國對台灣認知作戰的Q&A》](https://www.locuspublishing.com/book/detail/10514) (_Deceiving Taiwan is Better Than Attacking Taiwan: Q&A on China's Cognitive Warfare against Taiwan_) (Dakai Culture). The title itself is the argument. For the CCP, using military force is too expensive; deceiving Taiwanese people into not voting or voting incorrectly is far cheaper.[^19] This book was subsequently cited repeatedly in the political sections of CNA, _United Daily News_, and _Liberty Times_. These eight characters—"Deceiving Taiwan is Better Than Attacking Taiwan"—have become an abbreviation describing the CCP's strategy toward Taiwan.

## Three Million Black Bear Warriors

![Puma Shen speaking into a microphone at a podium, with a flag featuring the Black Bear Academy logo hanging on the wall behind him](/article-images/people/puma-shen-kuma-academy-lecture-2023.webp)

_In February 2023, Puma Shen delivers a lecture at the Black Bear Academy, with the academy's identification flag visible behind him. At the end of 2021, Puma Shen and Ho Cheng-hui, a researcher at the Taiwan Strategic Simulation Society, co-founded the [Black Bear Academy](https://kuma-academy.org/) (Kuma Academy).[^20] The academy's goal is "universal national defense education"—teaching every willing Taiwanese citizen skills that were originally reserved for soldiers or professional rescuers, such as civil defense, cybersecurity, fraud prevention, AED/CPR, wound dressing, and emergency evacuation response.[^21]

On September 1, 2022, Chao Ching-cheng—the former chairman of United Microelectronics Corporation (UMC) who had recently departed the Chinese market due to his advocacy for Taiwan's sovereignty—held a press conference. He announced that he would donate NT$600 million to the Black Bear Academy as part of a larger-scale donation plan to resist the CCP and protect Taiwan.[^22] At the press conference, Shen stated: "I hope every household will have a 'Black Bear Warrior' with relevant knowledge to protect their family's safety in times of crisis."[^23] The goal for the Black Bear Academy was set: **to train three million "Black Bear Warriors" within three years**.[^24]

What does three million represent? Taiwan's population is approximately 23 million; excluding children, the elderly, and those with mobility issues, three million is roughly one-fifth of the adult population. Chao Ching-cheng's NT$600 million is executed through a phased disbursement method—this money is designed as an audited project, not just a figure for a flashy press conference.[^25]

In September 2022, registration for the first basic camp of the Black Bear Academy quickly reached full capacity after opening.[^26] The curriculum includes fire safety, search and rescue, first aid, evacuation, information and communications security, information identification, and maintaining public order, supplemented by AED/CPR, injury assessment, and wound dressing.[^27] The outdoor training exercise "Blue Magpie Action" is held regularly, simulating the evacuation and medical assistance of non-combatants during wartime.[^28]

It is worth noting that Shen himself is not the legal representative of the Black Bear Academy. In a 2024 post on X, he wrote: "I wish I could be the person in charge of (Black Bear Academy), but the problem is the regulations and internal rules within the DPP; it means my family and I cannot serve as the person in charge."[^29] His roles as Deputy Executive Director of a Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) think tank and later as a legislator require him to maintain legal distance from the non-governmental organization he co-founded.

> **📝 Curator's Note**
> A universal defense system designed using a criminology framework: one in every seven adults knows how to save themselves, spot disinformation, and apply bandages during water or power outages. Shen's research describes how the enemy arrives; the Black Bear Academy's curriculum trains people on how to hold out after being attacked. Having the same person drive both endeavors creates a symmetry that feels unnervingly calm.

## The Sixth Time Within a Year

![Official portrait of Puma Shen, featuring his signature afro, round-rimmed glasses, wearing a white shirt and smiling](/article-images/people/puma-shen-legislator-portrait-2024.webp)

_Official portrait of Puma Shen upon assuming office as a member of the 11th Legislative Yuan in 2024. From researcher to legislator, the cost of disciplinary action has escalated from "naming a scholar" to "naming an elected legislator." Photo: Legislative Yuan. [Attribution via Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Puma_Shen_2024.jpg)._

On November 15, 2023, the Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) announced its list of at-large legislators for 2024, with Puma Shen ranked second, following children's rights advocate Lin Yue-chin. [^30] He was elected on January 13, 2024, and assumed office as a member of the 11th Legislative Yuan on February 1. [^4] He led the DPP caucus in promoting ten national security-related amendments (including the Act Governing Relations between the People of the Taiwan Area and the Mainland Area, the Act Governing Relations between the People of the Taiwan Area and the Regions of Hong Kong, Macao, and the Taiwan Area, the Nationality Act, the National Security Act, the Communication Security Protection Act, the Anti-Infiltration Act, the Cybersecurity Act, the Criminal Code, the Military Criminal Law, and the National Intelligence Work Act). [^31] Starting in 2025, he served as the convener of the Legislative Yuan's Foreign Affairs and Defense Committee. [^32]

However, on October 14 of that year, Chen Bin-hua, spokesperson for the Taiwan Affairs Office (TAO), announced that Puma Shen, Chao Ching-cheng, and the Black Bear Academy itself had been placed on a list of "stubborn Taiwan independence separatists," banning them and their family members from entering mainland China, Hong Kong, and Macao. [^33] This was the first time Puma Shen was named.

From that day forward, the escalation ladder climbed step by step. In a Facebook post on October 28, 2025, Puma Shen summarized the entire process in a single sentence: "**This is the 6th time within a year.**" [^34]

It took only thirteen months to complete these six steps. The legal basis cited in the Chongqing police report is the _Criminal Code_ combined with the CCP's June 2024 "Opinions on Punishing the Crimes of Splitting the State and Inciting Splitting the State by Stubborn Taiwan Independence Separatists" (commonly known as the "Twenty-Two Articles for Punishing Independence"). [^38] The police report even listed a reporting email `CQGACTD@163.com` and telephone number `023-65697660`, inviting anyone to provide "clues." [^39]

On the day the case was filed, Puma Shen responded on Facebook:

> "This is the 6th time within a year; this time, it's a direct criminal investigation by the police. The next step will likely be a wanted notice or trial in absentia; it doesn't matter, because '**Taiwanese people aren't afraid**.'" [^34]
>
> "**Eliminating those who raise problems and eliminating those who defend Taiwan—this is truly Communist Party style.**" [^34]

In June 2025, his father Shen Tu-cheng's company, Zhaoyi, was sanctioned by the CCP [^35], and one month later, the importer registration for the family business Sicuens was also canceled by customs [^36]; he wrote in a similar tone: "I am very honored to have been sanctioned three times in one year." [^40]

## CCTV's "Global Manhunt" Threat

Twelve days after the case was filed, on the evening of November 9, 2025, China Central Television (CCTV) News Channel aired a special feature titled "Exposing Shen Po-yang," lasting approximately seven and a half minutes (various Taiwanese media outlets reported it as eight minutes). CCTV claimed that Shen Po-yang used the Black Bear Academy to spread rhetoric that "China is about to launch a military takeover of Taiwan" and to train "violent Taiwan independence elements." The voiceover concluded with a warning tone: "**Stop now, or you will be next.**" [^41][^37]

At a press conference that day, the Mainland Affairs Council (MAC) defined this content as "transnational repression"—arguing that the goal was not to arrest him, but to force Taiwanese society into self-censorship before speaking out. [^42] That same night, Shen Po-yang posted on Facebook:

> "Can you stop doing exactly what I predict you're going to do every single time? **Have some creativity.**" [^43]
>
> "China is moving even faster, trying to use wanted notices to prevent me from leaving the country. **CCTV devoted a massive segment to a seven-and-a-half-minute pseudo-documentary about me, threatening a global manhunt.**" [^37]
>
> "Whether I am wanted or not, **nobody cares; Taiwanese people really don't give a damn about the CCP.**" [^37]

He also posted a short video with the camera pointed at a screenshot of CCTV: "Taiwan and China are **two separate sides, two different countries**. Don't dream of stretching your hands into Taiwan by fabricating charges." [^44]

International reactions gathered within two days. Maya Wang, Deputy Asia Director of Human Rights Watch, stated on October 31 that the Chongqing Public Security Bureau case was an application of Chinese law to exert transnational abuse of power. [^45] Multiple international human rights organizations voiced support simultaneously. The U.S. Department of State expressed public concern. [^47] Luke de Pulford, co-founder of the [Inter-Parliamentary Alliance on China](https://ipac.global/) (IPAC), posted in public support. [^48]

The Legislative Yuan also took action. On November 19, 2025, the Legislative Yuan's Foreign Affairs and Defense Committee (the same committee where Shen Po-yang himself serves as a convener) passed a resolution of condemnation proposed by Wang Ting-yu and others. In his remarks, Wang Ting-yu said: "**Even if opinions differ, one still has the right to be protected by the state and the Constitution.** The People's Republic of China has no right to carry out such political maneuvers against anyone based on these groundless reasons." [^49]

China has not yet officially placed Shen Po-yang on a bounty list similar to the Hong Kong version of the National Security Law; during the same period, the highest bounty was NT$5.3 million (RMB 250,000), issued for [YouTuber "Ba Jiong"](/en/people/pa-chiung-political-youtuber) and "Min-nan Lang." [^50] However, the combination of a "crime of splitting the state" filing and CCTV's "global manhunt" threat has pushed Shen Po-yang to a near-equivalent position.

## Screws at the Front Door

As the CCP escalates its actions, so too is the targeting of Shen Po-yang's private life.

In late August 2025, during an appearance on host Cheng Hung-yi's program 《話時代人物》 (Talking About Figures of the Era), Shen revealed for the first time that he is being followed "day in and day out." The most direct incident occurred right at his front door: a "screw" on a motorcycle was actually a pinhole camera, with the battery hidden under the seat and the exterior disguised as a screw. Shen photographed the license plate and called the police, but unexpectedly, the motorcycle vanished in less than an hour; tracking the license plate has since reached a dead end. [^51]

Even harder to digest were the threatening letters. The letters included a photo of Shen and his wife, Tseng Hsin-hui, with the **character "death" (死) written on Tseng Hsin-hui's clothing**, accompanied by threats to slit their daughter's throat. [^51] During the program, Shen stated: "This is extremely malicious, but this is exactly what the Communist Party does; we were mentally prepared for this." His wife Tseng Hsin-hui's reaction has been anxiety, insomnia, deteriorating health, and social phobia. [^51]

> **📝 Curator's Note**
> Shen Po-yang's daughter is not his biological child. He and his wife, Tseng Hsin-hui, adopted her through the Lixin Foundation; after a two-year process, Shen welcomed the thirteen-month-old baby girl in 2021. The couple uses the concept of a "tummy mama" to explain the role of the biological mother to the child. [^52]

## From Target to Candidate

In early April 2026, the Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) Nominating Committee convened. According to political reporting by [United Daily News](https://udn.com/), insiders revealed that the party intended to nominate Shen Po-yang to run for Mayor of Taipei. [^62] By mid-to-late April, this was almost a certainty. Former Minister of the Interior Hsu Kuo-yung gave his public endorsement. [^63] A TVBS poll from December 2025 showed that incumbent Taipei Mayor Chiang Wan-an had approximately 64% support, while Shen Po-yang had approximately 22%. [^64] Although he was trailing significantly, the ultimate outcome of the election is beyond the scope of this article.

On April 25, he cut off the afro hairstyle he had worn for over a decade, appearing on stage with Chiang Wan-an for the first time during the Songshan Cihui Temple Mother-Daughter Culture Festival. [^65] Yang Guo had cut his hair short.

On May 13, 2026, rumors became official. The DPP Central Executive Committee made the final decision: President Lai Ching-te, acting as Party Chairman, personally announced the mobilization of Shen Po-yang to run for Mayor of Taipei. Lai personally draped a campaign sash over him, and the two held hands while shouting "Chieh-suan" (victory). Lai stated that Shen possessed "the height of an international perspective, the breadth of cross-domain governance, and the warmth of concern for citizens," making him the "unrivaled choice for Taipei Mayor." [^66][^67] Accepting the sash, Shen Po-yang declared: "I have decided to run for Mayor of Taipei; please let me be your mayor." [^67] Election Day was set for November 28, with his opponent being the incumbent Chiang Wan-an. [^68]

At the time of his mobilization, the election prospects looked grim. A survey by the TVBS Poll Center on May 27 showed Chiang Wan-an at 58% and Shen Po-yang at 30%, a gap of 28 percentage points; even among the youth aged 20 to 29, Shen's 37% was lower than Chiang's 60%. [^69] International media described this confrontation as a challenge with low odds of success.

Five months later, the gap had closed, though "where" it closed depends on whom you ask. In the same series of surveys released by TVBS on October 8, Chiang Wan-an stood at 49% and Shen Po-yang at 41%, narrowing the gap from 28 percentage points to 8; in the 20–29 age group, the situation reversed, with Shen at 52% and Chiang at 44%, while 11% remained undecided. [^70] Conversely, a second wave of surveys released by the Xinmin Association on October 20 showed the opposite direction: Shen Po-yang at 50.3% and Chiang Wan-an at 33.3%, turning his September deficit of 1.2 percentage points into a 17-point lead. [^71] Because the two organizations used different samples and weighting methods, Kuomintang (KMT) Taipei City Councilors publicly questioned the impartiality of the latter; since the discrepancy between the two readings is greater than the sampling error of either individual poll, what can be said at this moment is that "the gap is narrowing," rather than "who is leading." From the bow of a boy mopping floors to the mockumentary on CCTV; from a thesis written at a desk to coordinates on a satellite map; from "not allowed to be the head of Black Bear Academy" to "officially running for Mayor of Taipei," Shen Po-yang's position has shifted several times. He used the same sentence to footnote every change: "I am a target." This statement is both self-deprecation and a calm academic observation. In the CCP's narrative regarding Taiwan, he is indeed the one being aimed at. It is rare for a scholar to take the label of their research subject, pin it onto themselves, and continue their work.

## Where is the Bowing Boy Now?

Back to that afternoon of mopping the floor. The moment Shen Po-yang froze, he stepped around the water streaks. The boy bowed to him. More than ten years later, he sits on the other side of a Chongqing Public Security Bureau police report; the word "death" has been slashed onto his wife's clothing, and the satellite coordinates near his home have been publicly called out by a Chinese Weibo account.

In June 2025, he wrote a very short sentence himself. On the day his father's company was sanctioned by the CCP, he responded on Facebook with four words: "**Extremely honored.**" [^40]

The question from that day at the juvenile detention center remains with him: "Why is there such a huge difference in how we treat people in different places?" He has studied this question for twenty years. Eventually, the subject of his research found him and marked him as prey. But he has studied long enough to know that **prey does not need to become a victim first**; they can first become the one asking questions—so from The Hague in the Netherlands to the German Bundestag and then to France, at every stop, he uses the same syntax to say the same thing: You can treat me this way, and I will continue my work.

In May 2026, he donned a campaign sash to fight another election. But on the CCP's satellite maps, the bullseye coordinates remain in the same place.
